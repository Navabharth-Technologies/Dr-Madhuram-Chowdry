Introduction

With advances in technology, Robotic Knee Replacement has emerged as one of the most precise and effective methods for knee replacement surgery. What is Robotic Knee Replacement?

Robotic knee replacement is an advanced form of knee replacement surgery that uses computer-assisted robotic technology to help the surgeon plan and perform the procedure with exceptional precision.

Contrary to popular belief, the robot does not perform the surgery independently. The surgeon remains in complete control throughout the operation. The robotic system acts as a highly accurate tool that assists the surgeon in achieving optimal implant positioning and alignment.

Who Needs Robotic Knee Replacement?

You may be a candidate for robotic knee replacement if you have:

  • Severe knee arthritis
  • Persistent knee pain despite medications
  • Difficulty walking or climbing stairs
  • Knee deformity (bow legs or knock knees)
  • Reduced quality of life due to knee pain
  • Failure of injections, physiotherapy, or other conservative treatments

Common Symptoms of Advanced Knee Arthritis

  • Pain while walking
  • Swelling around the knee
  • Morning stiffness
  • Grinding or clicking sounds
  • Difficulty sitting and standing
  • Reduced walking distance
  • Sleep disturbances due to pain

If these symptoms affect your daily activities, it may be time to consult a knee replacement specialist.

How Does Robotic Knee Replacement Work?

The robotic system creates a detailed three-dimensional model of your knee. This allows the surgeon to:

  • Assess your knee anatomy accurately
  • Plan implant size and position
  • Correct deformities precisely
  • Balance the knee ligaments effectively
  • Preserve healthy bone and soft tissue

During surgery, the robotic system provides real-time feedback, ensuring exceptional accuracy.

Advantages of Robotic Knee Replacement

  1. Greater Precision: Robotic technology allows precise implant placement, which may improve knee function and longevity.
  2. Better Alignment: Accurate alignment helps create a more natural-feeling knee.
  3. Less Soft Tissue Damage: The surgery can be performed with minimal disruption to surrounding tissues.
  4. Faster Recovery: Many patients begin walking within 24 hours after surgery.
  5. Reduced Pain: Improved surgical accuracy often leads to a smoother recovery experience.
  6. Improved Patient Satisfaction: Patients frequently report better knee function and confidence after robotic-assisted surgery.

Is Robotic Knee Replacement Safe?

Yes. Robotic knee replacement is considered a safe and reliable procedure when performed by an experienced orthopedic surgeon. Thousands of successful robotic knee replacements are performed worldwide every year with excellent outcomes.

How Long Does the Surgery Take?

Most robotic knee replacement procedures take approximately 60–90 minutes depending on the complexity of the case.

Recovery After Robotic Knee Replacement

Can Diabetic and BP Patients Undergo Surgery?

Yes. Patients with diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorders, and other medical conditions can safely undergo robotic knee replacement after proper medical evaluation and optimization. A multidisciplinary medical team ensures safety before, during, and after surgery.

How Long Does a Knee Replacement Last?

Modern knee implants can often last 20 years or more when implanted accurately and maintained appropriately. Factors affecting implant longevity include:

  • Weight management
  • Activity levels
  • Bone quality
  • Surgical accuracy
  • Implant quality

Cost of Robotic Knee Replacement in Mysore

The cost depends on various customized factors, including the type of implant chosen, the duration of the hospital stay, the general medical condition of the patient, and insurance coverage. A detailed clinical consultation helps determine the most suitable treatment plan and expected expenses.
