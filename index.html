Meet Dr. Madhuram Chowdry

MBBS, MS (Orthopaedics) • Senior Consultant Orthopedic & Knee Replacement Surgeon

Dr. Madhuram Chowdry is a Senior Consultant Orthopedic & Knee Replacement Surgeon and Medical Director at A.R. Hospital, Mysuru. He holds an MBBS, MS (Orthopaedics), Fellowship in Arthroplasty & Sports Medicine, with advanced training in robotic knee replacement at Narayana Health City, Bengaluru, and AO trauma care.

His clinical expertise includes robotic-assisted and minimally invasive knee replacement, subvastus knee replacement, arthritis & joint preservation, sports injuries and orthopedic trauma. Committed to advanced, evidence-based and personalised orthopedic care.

Believing firmly that surgical intervention is never the first option, Dr. Chowdry conducts thorough clinical and radiographic evaluations. Only when conservative therapies—such as structured physical therapy, lifestyle changes, and targeted joint injections—no longer yield relief, are modern surgical procedures considered.

Primary Surgical Specialty

Advanced Knee Replacement & Arthroplasty in Mysuru

Engineered for precision joint alignment, minimal tissue disruption, and rapid post-surgical mobility. Every knee solution is customized to individual patient anatomy and clinical need.

Total Knee

Total Knee Replacement (TKR)

Resurfacing damaged articular bone and cartilage with high-durability biocompatible implants. Indicated for severe stage 3-4 tricompartmental osteoarthritis where joint space is severely diminished.

  • Restores anatomical limb alignment & stability
  • Subvastus / muscle-sparing surgical approaches
  • Early weight-bearing rehabilitation protocols

Advanced Precision

Robotic-Assisted Knee Surgery

Computer-aided 3D virtual bone modeling and robotic instrumentation that assists the surgeon in achieving sub-millimeter implant positioning and optimal soft-tissue ligament balance.

  • Patient-specific 3D anatomical alignment
  • Preserves healthy surrounding bone and soft tissue
  • Reduced surgical blood loss & smoother recovery
Robotic Details → Consult
Joint Sparing

Partial Knee Replacement (PKR)

Ideal when wear is confined solely to the medial or lateral compartment. Replaces only the worn compartment while preserving the patient’s own cruciate ligaments (ACL/PCL) and natural knee kinematics.

  • Maintains natural joint feeling and flex
  • Smaller surgical incision and faster wound healing
  • Shorter hospital stay and quick return to routine
Indications → Consult
Complex Arthroplasty

Revision Knee Replacement

Specialized reconstructive surgery for previously operated knees facing implant loosening, wear, instability, or mechanical failure. Utilizes specialized stems, augments, and constrained prostheses.

  • Management of bone loss & joint instability
  • In-depth infection workup and structural assessment
  • Specialized implant systems for joint stabilization
Revision Guide → Consult
Keyhole Surgery

Knee Arthroscopy & Preservation

Minimally invasive keyhole visualization and repair of intra-articular structures including meniscus tears, loose chondral bodies, early synovial inflammation, and localized cartilage defects.

  • Diagnostic arthroscopy & meniscus debridement/repair
  • Joint debridement to delay knee arthritis progression
  • Day-care or minimal stay keyhole procedure
Arthroscopy → Consult
Recovery

Post-Operative Physiotherapy Protocol

Structured rehabilitation initiated within 24 hours of surgery. Focuses on quadriceps activation, joint range of motion (ROM), independent stair climbing, and gait re-training.

  • Multimodal pain management to support active mobilization
  • Continuous passive motion and targeted strengthening
  • Patient education on joint protection at home
