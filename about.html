Dr. Madhuram Chowdry is a Senior Consultant Orthopedic & Knee Replacement Surgeon and Medical Director at A.R. Hospital, Mysuru, and Aasare Hospital, Hunsur. He holds an MBBS, MS (Orthopaedics), Fellowship in Arthroplasty & Sports Medicine, with advanced training in robotic knee replacement at Narayana Health City, Bengaluru, and AO trauma care. His expertise includes robotic-assisted and minimally invasive knee replacement, arthritis management, sports injuries and orthopedic trauma.

Clinical Treatment Philosophy

  • 01
    Exhaust Conservative Therapy First Surgical intervention is never the first step. Targeted physiotherapy, weight balance, and intra-articular interventions are thoroughly evaluated first.
  • 02
    Sub-Millimeter Alignment & Bone Preservation Utilizing robotic assistance and minimally invasive tissue planes to preserve maximum bone stock and protect stabilizing collateral ligaments.
  • 03
    Early Mobilization & Dedicated Rehabilitation Multimodal pain relief protocols allow patients to bear weight safely within 24 hours of knee or hip replacement, reducing complication risks.
